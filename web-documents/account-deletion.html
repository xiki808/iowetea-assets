IOweTea — Account Deletion

This page describes how You can delete Your IOweTea account and what happens to Your data when You do. See also our Privacy Policy.

Delete your account in the app

  • Open Dashboard > Users > My Account.
  • Tap Delete Account, then confirm with Your password.

An owner can delete their account only while another owner remains. If You are the only owner of Your home, make another user an owner first.

What is deleted

When You delete Your account, the following is deleted:

  • Your home-server account
  • The VPN access for Your devices
  • Your Home Assistant login and its tokens
  • Your push notification tokens

The live account and its linked access are removed. A backup made before deletion can still contain an older copy of the account data until that backup is replaced or deleted. Copies of backups saved outside the home server remain with whoever saved them.

Home data the owners manage (devices, cameras, recordings) belongs to the home, not to Your account, and is not deleted with it.

Without the app

If You cannot use the app, You can delete Your account in one of these ways:

  • Ask an owner of Your home to remove You (Dashboard > Users > Remove).
  • By email: info.iowetea@gmail.com
