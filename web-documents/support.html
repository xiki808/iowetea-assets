IOweTea Support

For help with the IOweTea mobile app or your home server, email info.iowetea@gmail.com. Include your phone platform, app version, and a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords, VPN private keys, or Wi-Fi passwords.

Getting started

Open the app and follow Setup IOweTea to connect it to an IOweTea home server. If you do not have a server, select Try the demo on the setup screen to explore a sample home without an account.

Privacy and accounts

Read the privacy policy or the account-deletion instructions. You can also request account deletion by email at the address above.
