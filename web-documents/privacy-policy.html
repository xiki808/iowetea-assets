IOweTea Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how the IOweTea mobile app for Android and iOS, its home server, and the web interface shown in the app handle information. IOweTea is based in Malta. For privacy questions or requests, email info.iowetea@gmail.com.

How the app works

The app connects to an IOweTea server installed in your home. The server holds your account and home configuration and serves the main app interface. People who own or administer that home can manage its users, devices and settings. You can also try an included demo without creating an account or connecting to a home server; its sample data is reset when you leave the demo.

Information used by IOweTea

  • Account and access: The home server stores your username, password hash, role, language, login and notification settings, authentication tokens, and the credentials needed for your Home Assistant and WireGuard access. This information is used to sign you in, authorize access, and manage your account. Other authorized users of the same home may see your username and role where user management requires it.
  • Home setup and location: Setup uses the Wi-Fi network name and password you enter to connect the home server; the server stores those settings. The app checks network information to recognize your home Wi-Fi and decide how to connect. If you choose “Use my location” on the home map and grant permission, your device supplies a location fix. A location selected on the map is saved as your home's coordinates on the home server and can be used by home features such as weather. The app does not use your location for advertising.
  • Home activity and media: The home server and connected services hold the devices, rooms, remotes, security settings and events, camera feeds and recordings, and other information you configure or generate. The app displays this information and sends your commands to the home server. These records are shared with users you authorize for that home according to their access.
  • Camera and files: The app uses the phone camera when you choose to scan a Wi-Fi QR code. If you choose to create, download, export or restore a backup, the app handles the selected archive and sends a restore upload to your home server. Files you export or save outside IOweTea remain wherever you choose to keep them.
  • Notifications: If you enable notifications, the app obtains a push registration token and a device identifier and sends them, with the phone platform, to your home server. The server stores them with your account to send home alerts and account-related notifications. You can turn notifications off in the app. Notification permission alone does not register a token.
  • Connection and diagnostics: The app stores connection settings, including the home server address and VPN configuration, on the phone. The home server receives requests needed to provide the interface and may process connection details such as IP addresses and error information to operate and protect the service. Release builds do not enable the app's developer debug log.

VPN and remote access

With your permission, IOweTea can use a WireGuard VPN to reach your home server away from home Wi-Fi. You choose on-demand or always-on behavior and can disable the VPN. While it is connected, traffic covered by the VPN configuration travels through your home server; this can include internet traffic. IOweTea does not run a central VPN traffic relay. Your home server and the network services you visit can process traffic routed through them.

Other services and sharing

IOweTea uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, operated by Google, to deliver enabled push notifications. On iOS, Apple Push Notification service also participates in delivery. These services receive the information needed to route notifications, such as a registration token and notification content.

The home map requests tiles from OpenStreetMap's tile service. When you view or move the map, the tile service receives the map area requested and ordinary connection information such as the public IP address of the network used. If you choose to connect cloud devices or accounts through Home Assistant, the relevant provider processes information needed for that integration under its own privacy terms.

The home server also registers a server identifier and software version with IOweTea's public service to support setup, updates and certificates. That service may receive the server's public IP address through the connection. We do not use the information described here for third-party advertising or sell it to data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Security, storage and retention

Accounts and home records are stored on the home server. Passwords are stored as hashes. Remote access uses WireGuard encryption, and the app connects to the home interface over HTTPS. Access to home data depends on the account permissions set for that home. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Account information remains while the account exists. Home configuration, events and recordings remain according to the home's settings and its administrators' actions. The home owner can create an encrypted backup containing home data, including account records present when it was made. A new backup replaces the archive kept on the home server, but downloaded or otherwise copied backups are controlled by whoever holds them. Support emails and information you send us are kept as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations.

Your choices and deletion

You can deny or revoke camera, location, local network and notification permissions in your device settings. You can turn off notifications or disable IOweTea's VPN in the app. Some features will stop working without the relevant permission or connection.

To delete your account in the app, open Dashboard > Users > My Account > Delete Account and confirm with your password. If you are the only owner, another owner must be assigned first. Deletion removes your home-server account row, its push tokens and VPN peer, and your Home Assistant user and credentials. It does not delete shared home configuration, camera media or recordings, or copies in backups made before deletion. An owner can also remove another user. See the account-deletion instructions, or email info.iowetea@gmail.com if you cannot use the app.

To request access to or correction or deletion of other information, contact us at the email above. We will assess the request against the data held by IOweTea and the home owner, and any applicable legal obligations.

Children

IOweTea is intended for adults managing a home and is not directed to children.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by other appropriate means.
